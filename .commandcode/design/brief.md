# Design brief — WCP Compliance Agent V5

**Name:** WCP Compliance Agent V5
**Category:** Davis-Bacon payroll compliance audit console.
**User:** A compliance auditor or reviewer under time pressure, checking a WH-347 packet against wage determinations and arithmetic.
**Job:** Upload a payroll, inspect deterministic findings, review flagged coverage and make an attributable human decision.
**Artifact:** The payroll packet, the rule/source ledger, the findings, the decision and the audit trail.
**Evidence:** Deterministic rule results, wage-rate provenance, trust score, test counts, reproducible evidence, and explicit unsupported-coverage boundaries.
**Drift to refuse:** indigo/violet AI dashboard, gradient buttons, glass blur, glow orbs, holographic shield, equal feature cards, tiny labels, mouse-only navigation.
**Register:** Product. The console is the instrument; density and auditability outrank decoration.
